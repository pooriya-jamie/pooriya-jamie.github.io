### What this is

A benchmark proposal for studying how search and recommendation systems rank information in sensitive health contexts, especially when users arrive with different types of cancer-related intent.

### Benchmark focus

- **Medical queries:** information-seeking that looks clinical or treatment-oriented
- **Experience queries:** first-person, community, or lived-experience framing
- **Alternative queries:** searches that may surface contested or misleading health claims

### What it measures

The project compares rank exposure across trustworthy, caution-worthy, and problematic results. The goal is to make it easier to ask whether a system elevates reliable health information consistently, or whether certain query framings create disproportionate exposure to risky material.

### Why it matters

People do not search for health information in one clean way. A useful benchmark should reflect that messy reality and test whether ranking systems behave differently across medical, experiential, and alternative-health paths.
