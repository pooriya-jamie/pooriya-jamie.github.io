### What this is

A research project studying how search and recommendation systems rank information in sensitive health contexts, especially when users arrive with different types of cancer-related intent.

### Query focus

- **Medical queries:** information-seeking that looks clinical or treatment-oriented
- **Experience queries:** first-person, community, or lived-experience framing
- **Alternative queries:** searches that may surface contested or misleading health claims

### What it measures

The project compares rank exposure across trustworthy, caution-worthy, and problematic results. The goal is to ask whether a system elevates reliable health information consistently, or whether certain query framings create disproportionate exposure to risky material.

### Why it matters

People do not search for health information in one clean way. This project reflects that messy reality and tests whether ranking systems behave differently across medical, experiential, and alternative-health paths.
