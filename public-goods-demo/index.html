About this recording

Each period, 24 LLM agents receive 20 tokens apiece and form six new groups of four. Each agent chooses how much to keep and how much to share. This replay shows all 144 choices from one recorded session. The lines trace earlier group contacts.

The game follows Fowler & Christakis (2010). Persona labels describe the approach assigned to each agent.
