A small game. A larger question.

Why cooperation is a dilemma

01

Everyone starts equal.

Each agent receives 20 tokens. It can keep any amount and put the rest into a shared pool with three other agents.

02

Sharing helps the group.

Every contributed token returns 0.4 to each member—1.6 tokens across the group. But the contributor personally gets back less than it gave.

03

Experience travels.

Groups change every round. Agents see their previous group’s choices before deciding again. Can that experience shape how they treat new people?

About this demo

A window into the experiment.

This demo replays one recorded session of a public goods experiment using LLM agents with different assigned dispositions. The persona labels describe assigned prompts, not human participants.

The highlights show who interacted. They do not establish a causal effect. One session is an illustration; evidence about cooperation spreading requires analysis across sessions and checks of the estimator.

Research foundation

Inspired by Fowler & Christakis (2010), Cooperative behavior cascades in human social networks.
